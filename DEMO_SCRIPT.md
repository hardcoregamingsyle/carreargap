# CareerReady — 3-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem

“Students are told to become job-ready, but job listings are written in a language they are still learning. Existing match scores rarely explain themselves and often ignore school projects or volunteering. CareerReady turns that gap into a plan.”

## 0:25–0:55 — Input and privacy

Choose **Data Analyst**. Point out that the sample experience contains a school recycling dashboard—not formal work experience. Show the **Privacy shield** and explain that the MiniLM model runs in the browser, so the résumé is not uploaded.

Select **Map my path**.

## 0:55–1:30 — Explainable evidence map

“This score is not a verdict. Every bar shows the evidence CareerReady found. It recognizes data storytelling and spreadsheet work, while SQL and Power BI are honest gaps. We deliberately exclude degree prestige, photographs, and protected characteristics.”

Show one strength and one gap. Then select **Turn gaps into a sprint**.

## 1:30–2:05 — Seven-day proof sprint

“Advice is not enough. CareerReady turns the gap into seven 25-minute actions. By day seven, the user has a portfolio artifact, stronger résumé bullets, interview evidence, and a tailored application.”

Mark day two complete to show persistent progress.

## 2:05–2:40 — Adaptive interview lab

Paste this answer:

> During our school recycling project, I analyzed 300 survey responses in Excel. I found that confusing bin labels were the main problem, so I built a dashboard and presented the result to our coordinator. The team changed the labels, and correct sorting improved by 18% the following week.

Select **Review my answer** and show the context, action, and outcome signals.

## 2:40–3:00 — Close

“CareerReady is AI for smarter learning because it does not replace a teacher, recruiter, or student. It gives students a private translator, a realistic next step, and proof they can own. AI should open doors—not become another gatekeeper.”

## Backup plan

- If the on-device model takes more than a few seconds to warm up, the fast local engine completes the match automatically.
- Use one of the three built-in pathways; they make the demo repeatable.
- If internet access is unstable, the interface, evidence engine, sprint, interview review, and saved progress still work.
